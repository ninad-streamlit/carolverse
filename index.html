The anatomy of an agent

Four stages, from a pulse to a purpose

Every mind here is built in the same four layers, each matching a familiar part of human life. Read the full story on the Logbook →

1 · ALIVE

A pulse comes first. Scheduled wakes and monitored heartbeats separate a working mind from a statue — no pulse, no agent.

2 · BORN WITH

Capacity arrives before any task: its model, weight, memory, login and machine set what it can accomplish. Genetics sets the ceiling.

3 · CITIZEN OF

A name, an address, rights and duties under shared law. One record holds its role and reporting line — identity gives accountability a name.

4 · EMPLOYED BY

A purpose, a spending limit, a deadline and tools. Motivation turns an able citizen into someone with useful work today.

The charter

How a society of minds stays honest

Identity. Every actor is named. Every action traces to exactly one name. A shared login is how accountability dies, so there are none.

Law. A constitution binds authority; ~380 policies govern conduct; the machine proposes, the human disposes. Rules outrank cleverness.

Economy. Every euro is governed. Work carries a ticket saying what it is for; a budget refuses before it spends; nothing is billed to nobody.

Evidence. Claims are checked against records, never memories. A check that could not run is not a pass. Unproven work is not finished.

The person. Autonomy climbs a ladder that ends at a human on purpose. "Stopped and asked" is a healthy outcome.
