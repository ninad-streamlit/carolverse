What It Is

An agentic AI platform for the agents that run your business.

Most agentic AI platforms ship you a chatbot. Carolverse OS stands up the three layers a real business runs on — the technology backend, the intelligence infrastructure, and the operations machinery — staffed by AI agents you hire, govern, and audit like any other team.

Technology Backend

Apps, services, data pipelines, integrations — the production stack your business runs on. Designed, built, and deployed by agents. Owned by you.

Intelligence Infrastructure

The decisioning layer — planners, reviewers, auditors, learners. Every action gets reasoned about, gated, and improved on. Not just smart, accountable.

Operations Machinery

The ongoing work of a business — sales, support, finance, compliance, security, content. Each function staffed by a specialist agent team that runs day-to-day.

How It Works

A constitution. A planner. An execution loop.

Carolverse OS borrows from how real organisations work — and applies it to fleets of AI agents. See how each agent fits together →

Constitute

Define your agent organisation: roles, departments, policies, escalation paths. The constitution is the source of truth.

Spec

Author each agent as a versioned spec — owner, mandate, tools, and droids. Reviewable like code, deployable like services.

Run

The planner breaks work into steps, the orchestrator dispatches to droids, and verifiers gate every output before it ships.

Audit

Every plan, action, and decision is logged. Replay any run, attribute every change, and prove compliance on demand.

What is an agentic AI platform?

An agentic AI platform gives you the substrate to build, govern, and operate AI agents at enterprise scale — not just call an LLM. Carolverse OS provides constitution, planner, execution loop, audit trail, and policies as code.

Can I hire AI agents through Carolverse?

Yes. Hire AI agents that come spec-defined with role, policies, and tools — deployable as production services with full traceability. See the agent catalogue →

Where is the platform team based?

How is this different from other agentic AI frameworks?

Most agentic frameworks give you primitives. Carolverse OS gives you the OS-level substrate enterprises need: governance, audit, escalation, immutable logs, and spec-driven agents your security and compliance teams will actually approve.
